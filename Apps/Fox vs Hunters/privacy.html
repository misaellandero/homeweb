Fox vs Hunters Privacy Notice

Last updated: August 2, 2026

Fox vs Hunters is a local pass-and-play party game.

Data Collection

The app does not require account creation and does not use a custom backend. Game data such as saved players, categories, settings, and scores is stored on the device.

Foundation Models

On supported devices, the app may use Apple Foundation Models to generate category and word ideas in the selected game language. If this feature is unavailable, players can create categories manually.

External Display

External display mode shows public game information such as scoreboard, voting progress, round status, and reveals. Secret words remain private on the iPhone.
