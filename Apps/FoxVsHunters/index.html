Features

A social deduction game for one phone and a room full of players.

Secret word reveal

Each player reveals their card privately. Hunters see the word. Foxes do not.

Local multiplayer

No account, no matchmaking, no backend. Add players and pass one iPhone around.

Custom categories

Create word lists manually or use supported Apple Intelligence devices for category ideas.

Multiple foxes

Play with one or more hidden foxes and vote for the suspects each round.

Score tracking

Track hunter catches, fox escapes, voting accuracy, round winners, and top players.

Family friendly

Made for living room nights, classrooms, travel, family gatherings, and casual parties.

TV Mode

Make the scoreboard part of the room.

Use AirPlay or a compatible second display to show public game information on a TV: round status, voting progress, scores, and dramatic reveals. Secret words stay private on the iPhone.

How it plays

Simple rules, loud reactions.

1. Reveal

Players take turns holding the phone and revealing their secret role or word.

2. Discuss

Everyone gives clues without making it too obvious. Foxes try to blend in.

3. Vote

The group votes. Catch the fox to win, or let the fox escape for a dramatic upset.

Privacy

Built for play, not accounts.

Fox vs Hunters does not require account creation. Game data such as players, categories, settings, and scores is stored on the device. External display mode shows only public game information.
