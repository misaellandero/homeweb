DebtMe Privacy Notice

Last updated: July 16, 2026

DebtMe helps you track small loans and shared debts between friends and family.

Information You Add

The app may store names, amounts, notes, transaction status, and related records that you choose to enter.

Storage and Sync

DebtMe stores your records on your devices and may sync them through Apple services such as iCloud when enabled. The developer does not operate a custom backend for your debt records.

No Sale of Data

DebtMe does not sell, rent, or monetize your personal financial records.

Your Responsibility

You are responsible for the accuracy of the records you enter and for resolving debts outside the app.
