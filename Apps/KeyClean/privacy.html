KeyClean Privacy Notice

Last updated: July 16, 2026

KeyClean is a macOS utility by Misael Landero. It is designed to lock keyboard and trackpad input while you clean your Mac.

Data Collection

KeyClean does not collect, sell, rent, or share personal information. It does not include analytics, advertising SDKs, account creation, or a custom backend.

Keyboard Input

KeyClean uses macOS input-blocking APIs only to suppress accidental input while cleaning. The app does not record, store, transmit, or inspect the contents of your keystrokes.

System Permissions

KeyClean may request Accessibility or related macOS permissions so it can block input during cleaning mode. These permissions are managed by macOS in System Settings.
