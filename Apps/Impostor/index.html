Features

A social deduction game for one phone and a room full of players.

Secret Words

Everyone gets the same word except the foxes. Give clues and listen carefully.

Local Multiplayer

No matchmaking or backend. Add player names and pass one iPhone around.

Custom Categories

Create and edit your own categories and word lists for family, classes or parties.

AI Ideas

Supported devices can use Foundation Models to generate category and word ideas.

TV Scoreboard

Use AirPlay or a second display for public scores, voting progress and reveals.

No Account

Saved players, categories, settings and scores stay local on the device.

Technical Build

Local game logic with modern Apple platform features.

SwiftUI Architecture

Built with SwiftUI views, reusable components, view models, and dedicated service objects.

Game Engine

Separates rounds, phases, voting, scoring, player setup, and rule evaluation into focused engines.

Foundation Models

Generates category and word ideas on supported devices without requiring a custom backend.

External Display

Uses an external display scoreboard for AirPlay or TV play while keeping secret words on the phone.

Game Center + CloudKit

Includes service layers for high scores, sync, and entitlement plumbing.

ActivityKit + Haptics

Adds live discussion timing, background music, and tactile feedback for party play.

Privacy

A party game without accounts or backend.

Game data such as players, categories, settings and scores is stored locally on the device. External display mode shows public game information only; secret words remain private on the iPhone.
