Fox Vz Hunters Privacy Notice

Last updated: July 16, 2026

Fox Vz Hunters is a local pass-and-play party game.

Data Collection

The app does not require account creation and does not use a custom backend. Game data such as saved players, categories, settings, and scores is stored locally on the device.

Foundation Models

On supported devices, the app may use Apple Foundation Models to generate category and word ideas. If this feature is unavailable, you can create categories manually.

External Display

External display mode shows public game information such as scoreboard, voting progress, round status, and reveals. Secret words remain private on the iPhone.
