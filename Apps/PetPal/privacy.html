PetPal Privacy Notice

Last updated: July 16, 2026

PetPal helps you organize pet medical history, reminders, documents, and care records.

Information You Add

The app may store pet names, species, birth dates, medical events, reminders, notes, photos, and document attachments that you choose to enter.

Storage and Sync

Free Apple-platform use is designed around local storage and Apple sync services for devices using the same Apple ID. Premium cross-platform sync, when enabled, may use cloud backup services to sync your data across platforms.

No Sale of Data

PetPal does not sell your data. It does not use pet records for advertising.

Your Responsibility

You are responsible for the accuracy of medical records and reminders. PetPal is an organization tool and does not replace veterinary advice.
